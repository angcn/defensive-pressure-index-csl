# Variable Definitions

## Defensive Pressure Index Variables

| Variable | Meaning | Interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| `DPGZ` | Defensive possession gain zone | Location where the defending team regained possession. |
| `DPPBND` | Distance between the ball carrier and nearest defender | Smaller distances indicate more direct pressure on the ball carrier. |
| `DNUM` / `Defenders_Num` | Number of defenders around the ball carrier | Larger values indicate greater local defensive density. |
| `PL` | Length of the final attacking pass | Captures the opponent's immediate passing progression before sequence termination. |
| `PN` | Number of attacking passes before sequence termination | Captures the duration and continuity of attacking circulation. |
| `DLAODGL` | Distance from the least advanced outfield defender to the own goal line | Represents defensive line depth and vertical team organisation. |

## Outcome Variable

The defensive outcome was coded as an ordered variable:

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `F` | Unfavourable defensive outcome |
| `GP` | General possession gain / intermediate defensive success |
| `CDS` | Clear defensive success |

The exact operational definitions should follow the published article and original coding protocol.

## Contextual Variable

| Variable | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Running_score` | Score state at the time of the defensive sequence: losing, drawing, or winning. |
