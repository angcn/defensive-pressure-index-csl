# Article Information

## Published Article

Li, J., Wu, F., Li, A., & Martinez-Santos, R. (2026). Development and initial validation of a defensive pressure index using tracking data in the Chinese Super League. *Frontiers in Sports and Active Living, 8*, 1833549. https://doi.org/10.3389/fspor.2026.1833549

## Abstract Summary

This article developed and initially validated a Defensive Pressure Index (DPI) using tracking data from 30 Chinese Super League matches in the 2018 season. The analysis included 2,032 defensive sequences and examined whether DPI was associated with defensive outcomes and whether DPI differed across running score.

The study found that higher DPI values were significantly associated with more favourable defensive outcomes. DPI did not differ significantly across running score, suggesting that sequence-level defensive pressure was more closely related to local spatial-tactical organisation than to the global score state.
